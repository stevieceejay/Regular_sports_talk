01 Why this exists

Sports talk for the rest of us.

Tobias and I are regular guys who love sports. We talk about it like we get paid for it—but we don’t.

We’re building an AI chatbot that turns our questions into the honest, everyday reactions regular fans actually have. Anyone will be able to visit the site, try it out, and make it part of their own sports routine.

Our personal goal is to use it while streaming: regular guys talking sports with AI.
